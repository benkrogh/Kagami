Privacy & Security

Your ideas are safe here

Kagami has no cloud storage, no account, and no terms of service granting anyone rights to what you capture.

{{ICON:privShield}}

100% Local

Every capture, recording, and annotation is written straight to disk on your Mac. Nothing is ever uploaded anywhere.

{{ICON:privNoSignin}}

No Sign-In

No account, no email, no login screen. Just open Kagami and start capturing.

{{ICON:privNoTos}}

No Terms of Service

There's no fine print granting anyone rights to what you capture, because there's no service involved.

{{ICON:privCloudOff}}

Works Offline

No internet connection required. Kagami runs entirely on-device, so your captures stay yours, even offline.

Kagami is a gift to you

Start capturing your ideas right away, or fork the code and make your own improvements. It's free forever, because the future is generous.
